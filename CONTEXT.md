# Shared Home Context

## Glossary

- Home: A shared household space joined by multiple authenticated users.
- Member: A user who has joined the home and can create posts and vote on other members' posts.
- Purchase Request: A request post asking the home to approve a personal purchase. It can include an optional product link, description, and price.
- Request Vote: A member's approve or reject decision on a Purchase Request, optionally with a comment.
- Report: A report post about another member. It includes comments about the issue.
- Report Vote: A member's agree or disagree decision on a Report, optionally with a comment.
- Weekly Report Summary: A weekly count of Reports each member received, grouped by agreed and disagreed outcomes.
- Expiry Window: Purchase Request and Report details stay visible for 7 days, then expire to avoid storing too much detail.
